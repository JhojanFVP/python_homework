- Reflect on why websites use robots.txt and write 2-3 sentences explaining its purpose and how it promotes ethical scraping.
Robots.txt files are used by websites to specify to web spiders which areas of the site are accessible and which are not.
Site owners can stop automated bots from overwhelming servers and indexing sensitive content by posting these guidelines.
By adhering to the site owner's guidelines and ensuring equitable resource usage, robots.txt assists developers in engaging 
in ethical scraping.
